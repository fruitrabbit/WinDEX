PAPER & RESOURCES

WinDEX

Metric 3D Hand Motion Reconstruction in the Wild for Dexterous Teleoperation and Policy Learning

Read the paperPDFSupplementary videoMP4 ↓
Read the abstract +

WinDEX integrates geometric–temporal optimization, end-to-end fisheye-native reconstruction, and efficient distilled models to recover metric 3D hand motion from in-the-wild egocentric stereo videos. Visual–inertial SLAM places the motion in a world frame. Model-assisted annotation and retraining form a data–model flywheel, producing scalable supervision while keeping human-annotated 2D and optical motion-capture evaluation references independent. The resulting representations support reinforcement learning and real-time arm–hand teleoperation.
